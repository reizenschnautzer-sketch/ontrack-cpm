THE ONTRACK APPROACH

Understanding the whole study
Moving it forward, together

Clinical research brings together people with different responsibilities, pressures, and priorities. Moving a study forward takes an understanding of how their work connects, the judgment to choose an effective course of action, and the commitment to follow it through.

At OnTrack Clinical Project Management, these are the foundations of how we work.

01

EXPERIENCE

Decades of experience
Every perspective understood

Decades of hands-on experience have taught us to see a study through the eyes of everyone involved.

We understand the sponsor working toward a critical development milestone. The site balancing protocol requirements with the realities of patient care. The courier arriving to collect a time-sensitive shipment. The vendor coordinating that courier. The project assistant at a CRO making sure the right information reaches the right person.

We understand their constraints, their priorities, and what each needs from the others to do their job well. That perspective helps us recognise where expectations diverge, where handoffs break down, and where a seemingly minor misunderstanding can become a major delay.

We use that understanding to help people work together. We translate competing priorities into clear responsibilities, practical workflows, and shared expectations. We build a process in which everyone understands both their own role and how it contributes to the study.

We help everyone speak the same language  –  and move the project forward together.

02

COMMITMENT

The best possible strategy
Full commitment to delivery

Ambitious development programmes demand ambitious targets. We understand why those targets are set, and we take them seriously.

We cannot promise that every problem will have a solution or that every deadline will be met. We can make a clear commitment: we will pursue the strongest achievable outcome with all the expertise, judgment, and effort the situation requires.

That means examining the options, identifying what will make the greatest difference, and putting resources where they can have the most impact. It means challenging assumptions, anticipating consequences, and adjusting the strategy when circumstances change. It also means recognising when persistence is needed  –  and when a different approach will achieve more.

We take responsibility for the strategy we recommend and for driving its execution. When a target is at risk, we make the situation clear, bring forward alternatives, and act. You will know what we are doing, why we believe it is the best course, and what decisions are needed from you.

Our commitment is to leave no worthwhile option unexplored and no avoidable obstacle unaddressed.

03

PARTNERSHIP

Your study
Our shared priority

Drug development is a vast, demanding industry, with countless programmes competing for resources and attention. For a smaller sponsor, securing the response its study needs from a large organisation can take considerable effort and persistence.

We understand that challenge. We know how to work with organisations of every size, from independent specialists to global laboratory providers and technology companies. Whether your study depends on a major global central laboratory or one of the world’s largest EDC systems, we understand how to navigate complex organisations, reach the right people, escalate effectively, and follow through.

We bring that same commitment to our relationship with you. You have direct access to experienced people who know your study and understand why it matters. We make time for the conversation. We come to your office when working through an issue together will make a difference. We stay engaged when a task becomes difficult or crosses organisational boundaries.

When something has not happened, our responsibility is to establish why, agree on what comes next, and drive it forward. An explanation is the starting point for action.

We work as your partners, with a personal stake in earning your trust and helping your programme succeed.
